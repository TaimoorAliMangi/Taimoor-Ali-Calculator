# App roles and personas

Updated 13 October 2026. Revised UAT verified locally; cloud checks pending. Public role views reorder actual result blocks: borrower monthly payments first, family adviser equity and annual totals first, and housing-finance adviser rates and schedule first. They do not change permissions or financial rules. English and Urdu preserve the same scenario. No identity data is collected.

## R1 Prospective borrower

A prospective borrower is a Pakistani first-time homebuyer assessing a household commitment. They can choose borrower guidance, adjust property, principal, tenure, equity and KIBOR, then inspect repayments. They must never receive guaranteed eligibility, approval or future rates.

Persona hypothesis: a salaried or self-employed household with limited savings needs clear monthly payments and upfront equity. This is research-informed, not an interview.

## R2 Family adviser

A family adviser is a household member helping compare housing commitments. They can choose family guidance, change public inputs and inspect yearly balances. They must never access another person's saved financial data; the app stores none.

Persona hypothesis: a relative needs clear totals for a shared budget discussion. This role is inferred rather than interviewed.

## R3 Housing finance adviser

A housing finance adviser is a bank professional explaining an indicative scenario. They can choose adviser guidance and explain shared calculator outputs. They must never present an estimate as a sanctioned bank offer or edit scheme rules.

Persona basis: SBP requires participating institutions to communicate scheme features through branches and other channels.

## Presentation and evidence

The principal slider spans PKR 1m–10m in PKR 100,000 steps. Urdu uses RTL; switching preserves role, values and results. Eligibility appears below the calculator. Credit: KnowledgeCity; independent planning tool.

Core eligibility is first-time Pakistani homebuyers with valid CNIC and no housing unit. Age and income vary by bank; PKR 50,000 is not a universal minimum.
