Check your eligibility

Applicants generally need a valid CNIC or eligible NICOP/POC and must be first-time homeowners with no housing unit in their name. Age 18 or above is an example bank minimum; age at loan maturity is bank-specific. Income requirements vary by bank: PKR 50,000 monthly income is a planning example, not a universal scheme threshold.
